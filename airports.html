Chicago Airports

Chicago is served by two major airports, both with convenient access to downtown Chicago and the conference venue:

O'Hare International Airport (ORD)

Distance from venue: Approximately 18 miles (29 km), 45 minutes travel time
Address: 10000 W O'Hare Ave, Chicago, IL 60666

Transportation Options:

  • CTA Blue Line: $5 fare to downtown Chicago. Trains run 24/7 with a 45-minute ride to downtown.
  • Cab/Rideshare: $40-$60 depending on traffic, about 35-60 minutes.
  • Airport Shuttle: Various services available, typically $30-40 per person.

Midway International Airport (MDW)

Distance from venue: Approximately 12 miles (19 km), 30 minutes travel time
Address: 5700 S Cicero Ave, Chicago, IL 60638

Transportation Options:

  • CTA Orange Line: $2.50 fare to downtown Chicago. Trains run from 4:00 AM to 1:00 AM, with a 25-minute ride to downtown.
  • Cab/Rideshare: $30-$50 depending on traffic, about 25-40 minutes.
  • Airport Shuttle: Various services available, typically $25-35 per person.

Tips for Airport Travel

  • Allow extra time during peak travel hours for potential traffic congestion, especially from O'Hare.
  • For public transit, purchase a Ventra card at airport stations for easier travel throughout the city.
  • Both airports offer rideshare pickup zones in designated areas.
