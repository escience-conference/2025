Welcome to Chicago

Chicago, known as "The Windy City," is the third-largest city in the United States and one of the world's great urban centers. Located on the shores of Lake Michigan, Chicago offers world-class architecture, culinary experiences, museums, and cultural attractions.


Quick Facts

  • Population: Approximately 2.7 million (city proper), 9.5 million (metropolitan area)
  • Founded: 1837
  • Nicknames: The Windy City, Second City, City of Big Shoulders
  • Known for: Architecture, deep-dish pizza, blues music, sports teams, and lakefront
  • Time Zone: Central Standard Time (CST)

Weather

Chicago in late August typically experiences average high temperatures of 80-85°F (27-29°C) and lows of 65-70°F (18-21°C). Days are usually warm and can be humid, while evenings are generally pleasant. However, the weather can be variable, so it's advisable to check the forecast before your trip and pack accordingly.

Local Transportation

Chicago has an extensive public transportation system called the CTA (Chicago Transit Authority), which includes:

  • L Trains: The elevated train system with color-coded lines connecting most parts of the city
  • Buses: Extensive network covering areas not served by the L
  • Water Taxis: Available along the Chicago River and to Museum Campus
  • Divvy Bikes: Bike-sharing system throughout the city

Consider purchasing a Ventra Card for convenient access to public transportation during your stay.
